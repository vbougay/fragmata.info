This unit stands in the area of the new Limassol port. Its water goes straight into the network of the Limassol District Local Government Organisation (EOA), which runs the drinking-water supply in and around the city.

It was one of the first four mobile units in the government's drought programme of late 2024, which planned it at 5,000 m³ a day, expandable to 10,000, at a capital cost of about €4.2 million. The contract was signed in August 2025 with a four-month build period and a January 2026 target. The unit ran on a trial basis from the end of March 2026, until the contract's acceptance steps were complete, and the ministry announced full operation on 5 April 2026. The contractor, the contract value and the price per m³ have not been published.

Sources also disagree about what the unit treats. The December 2024 plans described a seawater unit, while a ministry release of June 2026 said it uses brackish water from boreholes.
