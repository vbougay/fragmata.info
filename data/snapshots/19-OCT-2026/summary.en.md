
### Recent Changes (October 16 — 19, 2026)

Monday's bulletin (covering the weekend): total storage **35.3%** (102.75 MCM) — down 0.04 MCM from Friday (102.79 MCM) after two small gains. Three-day inflow was 0.27 MCM, bringing the season total to 1.28 MCM. [Arminou](/dam/arminou/) keeps climbing, to 66.1% (+1.2pp), and [Kouris](/dam/kouris/) added 0.05 MCM (42.26 MCM, 36.7%). [Kalopanagiotis](/dam/kalopanagiotis/) remains the only dam overflowing at 100%. The gap over last year stands at **24.0 points**.

**Notable movements (vs. October 16):**
- [Arminou](/dam/arminou/) **66.1%** (+1.2pp) — the only Southern Conveyor dam still rising, 0.07 MCM of inflow
- [Kouris](/dam/kouris/) **36.7%** — +0.05 MCM on 0.14 MCM of inflow, the largest single inflow
- [Agia Marina](/dam/agia-marina/) **49.3%** (+0.6pp) and [Argaka](/dam/argaka/) **44.5%** (+0.2pp)
- [Pomos](/dam/pomos/) **58.8%** (−0.5pp) — the biggest slip among the Chrysochou dams
- [Achna](/dam/achna/) **8.0%** — unchanged, zero inflow
- [Kalopanagiotis](/dam/kalopanagiotis/) **100%** — still the only dam overflowing
