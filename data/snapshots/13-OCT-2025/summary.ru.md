
### Recent Changes (October 10 - 13, 2025)

**General Overview:**
The total water storage in Cyprus reservoirs decreased slightly from 11.8% to 11.7% between October 10 and 13, 2025. This minor decline of 0.1 percentage points continues the trend of ongoing consumption with minimal inflows. The total inflow for the 2025/26 period remains minimal at 0.086 MCM.

**By Region:**
- **Southern Conveyor:** Slight decrease from 12.5% to 12.4%, with Kouris declining marginally from 12.471 to 12.298 MCM (from 10.8% to 10.7%).
- **Paphos:** Slight decrease from 10.4% to 10.3%, with Asprokremmos dropping from 5.298 to 5.213 MCM.
- **Chrysochou:** Remained stable at 11.9-12.0%.
- **Nicosia:** Slight decrease from 2.2% to 2.2%.

**Assessment:**
Inflows remain minimal. The only notable inflow was recorded at Kalavasos (0.002 MCM in the last 24 hours). Consumption continues normally as we await the start of the rainy season.
