
### Recent Changes (November 24 - December 5, 2025)

**General Overview:**
Total water storage in Cyprus reservoirs remained relatively stable at approximately 9.4% between November 24 and December 5, 2025. Inflows remain minimal, with total inflow of 0.327 MCM for the 2025/26 period. December inflows have started with just 0.032 MCM.

**By Region:**
- **Southern Conveyor:** Slight decline, with Kouris at 9.311 MCM (8.1%) from 9.9 MCM.
- **Paphos:** Relatively stable, with Asprokremmos at 4.123 MCM (7.9%). Mavrokolympos rose to 6.7%.
- **Chrysochou:** Stable, with Evretou at 2.453 MCM (10.2%).
- **Nicosia:** Kalopanagiotis increased to 25.9% due to local inflows.

**Assessment:**
Storage levels remain significantly lower than last year (9.4% vs 25.4%). December inflows have just begun. Winter rainfall is expected to replenish reservoir levels.
