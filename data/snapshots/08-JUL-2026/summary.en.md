
### Recent Changes (June 30 — July 8, 2026)

Total storage at **41.3%** (120.1 MCM) — just -1.2 MCM over eight days. This remains the slowest drawdown pace seen at this point in the summer for years. The gap over last year holds at **+22.5pp**. Season total inflow: **114.6 MCM** (July: 0.39 MCM). [Arminou](/dam/arminou/)→[Kouris](/dam/kouris/) transfer: **20.44 MCM** since October.

**Notable movements:**
- **[Arminou](/dam/arminou/) 56.2%** (+1.6pp since June 30) — new season high, the lone riser; light inflows keep outpacing pumping to [Kouris](/dam/kouris/)
- **[Mavrokolympos](/dam/mavrokolympos/) 75.8%** (-4.2pp since June 30) — the week's biggest decliner; started the year at 0%
- [Klirou-Malounta](/dam/klirou-malounta/) **96.4%** — below full since early July; only [Kalopanagiotis](/dam/kalopanagiotis/) still overflows
- [Kouris](/dam/kouris/) **41.2%** (47.4 MCM) · [Kannaviou](/dam/kannaviou/) **50.4%** — negligible change
- [Achna](/dam/achna/) **1.9%** — zero inflow all season, no recovery expected before autumn
