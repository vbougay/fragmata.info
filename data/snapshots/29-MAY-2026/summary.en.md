
### Recent Changes (May 21 — May 29, 2026)

Total storage at **42.4%** (123.3 MCM), up from 41.8% (121.8 MCM) on May 21 — **+0.6pp, +1.5 MCM in 8 days**. New season high. **+20.8pp above last year** (21.6%). Season total: **110.8 MCM** — record since 1987. [Arminou](/dam/arminou/)→[Kouris](/dam/kouris/) transfer: **19.93 MCM**.

**Notable movements:**
- **[Arminou](/dam/arminou/) 42.8%** — new season high (+6.8pp in 8 days); transfer to [Kouris](/dam/kouris/) now 19.93 MCM
- **[Germasoyeia](/dam/germasoyeia/) 60.3%** — season high, highest since 2020
- **[Evretou](/dam/evretou/) 50.1%** — holding above 50%
- [Kannaviou](/dam/kannaviou/) **52.3%** — near season high, +29.2pp above last year
- **10 dams at 100%** including [Tamassos](/dam/tamassos/), [Klirou-Malounta](/dam/klirou-malounta/), [Solea](/dam/solea/)
- [Achna](/dam/achna/) **1.8%** — zero inflow all season
