
### Recent Changes (March 4 — 13, 2026)

Total storage at **21.2%** (61.6 MCM), steady from 21.0% (61.1 MCM) on Mar 4. Total 25/26 inflow now 42.0 MCM — more than double last year's full season (18.7 MCM). March has been quiet so far with minimal rainfall.

**Notable movements:**
- Kouris +0.4 MCM (19.6%) — steady inflows via Arminou transfer (7.27 MCM since October)
- Evretou at 26.8% (6.431 MCM) — surpassed last year (25.6%) and annual max (6.201)
- Solea, Klirou-Malounta, Kalopanagiotis, Pomos remain at 100%
- Southern Conveyor stable, minor dips at Lefkara (10.9%) and Polemidia (37.5%)

Despite gains, storage remains below last year (25.2%). The 10% island-wide water supply cut stays in effect.
