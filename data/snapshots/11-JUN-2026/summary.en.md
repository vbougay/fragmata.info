
### Recent Changes (June 2 — 11, 2026)

Total storage at **42.4%** (123.2 MCM) — **13th straight day unchanged**, but individual dams are beginning to ease as the dry season takes hold. **+21.6pp above last year** (20.8%). Season total inflow: **112.8 MCM**. [Arminou](/dam/arminou/)→[Kouris](/dam/kouris/) transfer: **20.32 MCM** since October.

**Notable movements:**
- **[Evretou](/dam/evretou/) 49.9%** — just slipped below 50% for the first time in weeks as summer drawdown accelerates
- **[Arminou](/dam/arminou/) 47.7%** — 0.04 MCM/day inflow sustains level despite ongoing pumping to [Kouris](/dam/kouris/)
- **[Mavrokolympos](/dam/mavrokolympos/) 82.5%** — steady summer decline from peak; was 0% last year
- [Kouris](/dam/kouris/) **41.9%** (48.2 MCM) · [Germasoyeia](/dam/germasoyeia/) **59.8%** — easing slightly from season highs
- **4 dams at 100%** — [Pomos](/dam/pomos/), [Kalopanagiotis](/dam/kalopanagiotis/), [Klirou-Malounta](/dam/klirou-malounta/), [Tamassos](/dam/tamassos/) (99.8%), [Solea](/dam/solea/) (98.9%)
- [Achna](/dam/achna/) **2.0%** — zero inflow all season
- Cabinet approved extra **3.5 MCM** for irrigation on June 9: 2.0 MCM for Paphos, 1.5 MCM for Southern Conveyor
