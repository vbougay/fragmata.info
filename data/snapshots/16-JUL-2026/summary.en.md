
### Recent Changes (July 15 — July 16, 2026)

Total storage held at **40.9%** (118.8 MCM) — essentially unchanged from 40.9% yesterday, keeping the slowest summer drawdown pace in years. The gap over last year holds near **+22.8pp**. Season total inflow: **115.2 MCM** (July so far: 0.83 MCM). The [Arminou](/dam/arminou/)→[Kouris](/dam/kouris/) transfer remains at **20.44 MCM**, unchanged.

**Notable movements:**
- [Mavrokolympos](/dam/mavrokolympos/) **72.6%** (-0.7pp) — the largest single-day drop
- [Argaka](/dam/argaka/) **87.5%** (-0.5pp) and [Pomos](/dam/pomos/) **88.6%** (-0.5pp) — easing off near-full levels
- [Kalopanagiotis](/dam/kalopanagiotis/) **100%** — still the only dam overflowing
- [Achna](/dam/achna/) **1.9%** — unchanged, zero inflow all season
