
### Recent Changes (April 20 — April 29, 2026)

Total storage at **39.1%** (113.7 MCM), up from 37.9% (110.2 MCM) on Apr 20 — **+3.5 MCM in 9 days**. New season high. **16.0pp above last year** (23.1%). Season total: **97.4 MCM** — April 2026 at 22.9 MCM remains the **2nd highest April on record**. [Arminou](/dam/arminou/)→[Kouris](/dam/kouris/) transfer: 16.88 MCM.

**Notable movements:**
- **[Germasoyeia](/dam/germasoyeia/) 55.1%** — new season high, crossed 55% for the first time (+2.5pp since Apr 20). Was at just 14% eight weeks ago
- **[Arminou](/dam/arminou/) 41.9%** (+6.6pp since Apr 20) — new season high, recovering strongly despite 16.88 MCM pumped to [Kouris](/dam/kouris/) since October
- [Kouris](/dam/kouris/) 37.1% (42.6 MCM, +1.2pp) — new season high, 18.1pp above last year
- [Polemidia](/dam/polemidia/) 59.0% — new season high, 21.5pp above last year
- 9 dams at 100%; [Achna](/dam/achna/) remains 2.2% — zero inflow all season
