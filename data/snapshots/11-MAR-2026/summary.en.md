
### Recent Changes (March 2 — 11, 2026)

Total storage at **21.1%** (61.4 MCM), up from 20.6% (59.9 MCM) on Mar 2. Total 25/26 inflow now 41.7 MCM — more than double last year's full season (18.7 MCM). The pace of gains has slowed as March continues with lighter rainfall.

**Notable movements:**
- Kouris +0.7 MCM (19.5%) — steady inflows via Arminou transfer (7.23 MCM since October)
- Evretou at 26.7% (6.405 MCM) — continues above annual max (6.201)
- Solea, Klirou-Malounta, Kalopanagiotis at 100% — full
- Rest of Southern Conveyor stable, minor losses at Kalavasos and Lefkara

Despite gains, storage remains below last year (25.3%). The 10% island-wide water supply cut stays in effect.
