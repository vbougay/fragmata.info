
### Recent Changes (April 15 — April 22, 2026)

Total storage at **38.2%** (111.2 MCM), up from 36.8% (107.2 MCM) on Apr 15 — **+4.0 MCM in 7 days**. New season high. **14.7pp above last year** (23.5%). Season total: **94.4 MCM** — April 2026 at 19.9 MCM remains the **2nd highest April on record** (behind only 2018/19's 25.3 MCM). [Arminou](/dam/arminou/)→[Kouris](/dam/kouris/) transfer: 16.55 MCM.

**Notable movements:**
- **[Vyzakia](/dam/vyzakia/) at 96.4%** (+7.3pp since Apr 15) — new season high, overflow imminent, 34× last year's 2.8%
- **[Germasoyeia](/dam/germasoyeia/) 52.6%** (+3.3pp) — third day above 50%, new season high, 27.4pp above last year
- **[Polemidia](/dam/polemidia/) 57.2%** (+1.7pp) — new season high, 19.7pp above last year
- [Kouris](/dam/kouris/) 36.2% (41.7 MCM, +2.3 MCM) — new season high, 17.0pp above last year
