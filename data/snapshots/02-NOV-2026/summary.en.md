
### Recent Changes (October 26 — November 2, 2026)

Monday's bulletin (covering the weekend): total storage **35.1%** (101.97 MCM) — essentially unchanged since Friday (+0.01 MCM) and up 0.05 MCM on the week, as the rain has delivered only small inflows. Inflow over the last 24 hours was 0.07 MCM, bringing the season total to 2.36 MCM. The gap over last year is **24.5 points**.

**Notable movements (vs. October 26):**
- [Arminou](/dam/arminou/) **71.0%** (+3.2pp on the week, 3.05 MCM) — still climbing, the only Southern Conveyor dam rising steadily
- [Mavrokolympos](/dam/mavrokolympos/) **32.3%** (+2.6pp on the week) — the biggest rise
- [Kouris](/dam/kouris/) **36.4%** (41.87 MCM) — 0.04 MCM of inflow, steady
- [Asprokremmos](/dam/asprokremmos/) **35.2%** (18.42 MCM) — steady
- [Achna](/dam/achna/) **7.8%** — zero inflow
- [Kalopanagiotis](/dam/kalopanagiotis/) **100%** — still the only dam overflowing
